§1

Business context & objective

The user is a solo Indian F&O trader (Nifty and Bank Nifty index options, plus stock F&O). Their decisions cluster at the pre-open (09:00–09:08 order entry) and the 09:15 open. Trades are rechecked around midday and reviewed after the 15:30 close.

What moves their book: overnight US and Asia sessions, GIFT Nifty, crude, USD/INR, DXY, US yields, FII/DII flows, OI, PCR and max pain, India VIX, the F&O ban list, scheduled events (RBI, Fed, CPI, results), geopolitics (shipping lanes, sanctions, conflict, tariffs), tech news (AI capex and semiconductors feed Nasdaq, which feeds Nifty IT), and Bitcoin as a gauge of risk appetite.

Objective

One trusted source for every trading decision

Four on-time, fact-checked reports each trading day, cross-market charts, and a citation on every claim. The trader stops researching and spends that time deciding.

Business objective (later)

A subscription product traders will pay for

Prove the verification engine on one demanding user, then package it for retail F&O traders and investors in India. The paid promise is trust: every claim cited.

GoalTargetWhy it matters
Pre-market research time≤ 15 minFrees the 08:45–09:15 window for decisions.
Claims with a working citation100%The product's core promise.
Unverified claims published0One wrong number costs more trust than ten right ones earn.
Reports delivered on time≥ 98%A late morning report is a useless morning report.
Non-goals

Placing orders. Buy/sell calls or price targets in Citebell's own voice (third-party views appear only with attribution). Tick-level data for scalping. Social media as a source.

§4

How Citebell is different

Every competitor either has trust without synthesis (wires, terminals) or synthesis without proof (apps, AI summaries). Citebell's position is synthesis with proof, delivered at fixed times in the trading day.

Trust

Verification-first

Each claim goes through source tiers, corroboration, a numeric check, a recency check and a publish gate. Anything unverified never appears.

Proof

Citations that show their work

Every citation carries a link, publisher, timestamp, matched quote, source tier and an archived snapshot, so link rot and silent edits can't break it.

Numbers

Figures come from data, not articles

Figures come from exchange and primary feeds and are checked against a second feed. A number has one value everywhere in a report, which fixes P3.

Lens

Written for F&O

Each story is tagged with the index or sector it affects, likely direction and time horizon, plus a one-line cause → effect.

Rhythm

Built around the trading day

Four fixed drops in IST. Each one opens with what changed since the last report.

Transmission

Global → India map

Nasdaq semis → Nifty IT. Crude → OMCs, paints, aviation. DXY → INR → FII flows. BTC → risk appetite.

Memory

Learns with the trader

Story timelines, a trade journal that links news to trades, and descriptive pattern statistics (never signals).

Integrity

Public corrections log

No ads, no paid placements, no unattributed tips. Every correction is timestamped and visible.

Brand: name & identity

citebell[1]

Verified before the bell.

A bell, and a citation mark in its corner. The [1] in the wordmark is the same citation marker that appears on every claim in the product: the brand device and the core UI element are one thing.

Recommendation: Citebell

  • Meaning. Cite is the product's promise: every claim cited. Bell is the market's opening bell and the four scheduled drops.
  • Sound. Two short syllables, easy to say in English and Hindi. It works as a verb: “Citebell it before you trade.”
  • Room to grow. Not tied to F&O or India. It stretches to equities, crypto and global investors, with tiers like Citebell Pro and Citebell Desk.
  • Availability. .com, .app, .in, .ai and .io were all unregistered at check time. A web search found no company or product using the name.
  • Risk to manage. “Cite” sounds like “site” and “sight”. The logo and exact-match domains carry the spelling, and the pun on “sight” (seeing clearly) doesn't hurt.

Verification pipeline

Extract

Split the draft into atomic claims: number, event, forecast or opinion.

Tier sources

T1 primary · T2 established press · T3 context only · TX never publishable.

Corroborate

Find independent support. Syndicated PTI or Reuters copies count as one source.

Reconcile numbers

Match against T1 data or a second feed within tolerance.

Check links

HTTP 200, the claim text is on the page, publish time captured, snapshot archived.

Check recency

Reject stale or resurfaced stories; stamp every number with its as-of time.

Gate

Pass → publish with a badge. Fail → withhold and log. Nothing unverified gets through.

Market numberMatches T1 data, or two independent feeds agree within tolerance (index ±0.01%, flows exact to ₹0.01 Cr). Always shows an as-of time.Primary data
News eventOne T1 source, or two independent T2 sources.Verified · 2 sources
Forecast or opinionAttributed to its author (“Brokerage X expects …”) with a link. Never in Citebell's own voice.Attributed view
Fails any ruleLeft out and written to the audit log. If a whole section misses its deadline, a “withheld” banner shows the reason and the retry time.Withheld

§8

Phasing: V0 → Nirvana

Each phase has to pass its exit gate before the next one starts. Trust is earned first, then scope grows.

V0 · Proof of trust

~3 weeks
  • Four scheduled reports as HTML/PDF, sent to Telegram and email
  • About 15 curated sources; verification gate v1 (tiers, link check, match against one feed)
  • A citation on every claim; a “flag as wrong” button on each claim
  • Simple archive page; Markets page built on chart widgets
Exit gate20 trading days in a row: ≥95% on time, zero unverified or wrong claims found in spot checks, and baseline research time measured.

V1 · Console

~6 weeks
  • Full web app: Today, Reports, Markets, Flows, Crypto & Tech, Calendar
  • “What changed” view, story clustering, verification badges, fact-check drawer
  • Corrections log, search and archive, derivatives panel, economic, results and expiry calendars
  • Verified breaking alerts; second-feed reconciliation
Exit gateResearch time ≤ 15 min; usefulness rating ≥ 4.2/5.

V2 · Analyst

next quarter
  • Ask Citebell: answers only from the verified corpus, with citations
  • Story timelines; impact tags with historical analogues
  • Trade journal (manual entry or tradebook import, read-only); watchlist ranking
  • 2-minute audio brief; weekly review
Exit gate≥ 70% of trades have a linked rationale.

V3 · Co-pilot + SaaS beta

after V2
  • Descriptive pattern library; scenario planner (crude +5% → sector sensitivity)
  • IV and OI shifts overlaid on news times; read-only broker positions → news on held stocks
  • Accounts, onboarding, plans and billing; licensed data for redistribution; legal review of SEBI research-analyst rules
Exit gatePrimary source on ≥ 90% of days; 20 beta users meet the trust guardrails.

Nirvana

the end state
  • A personal trading-intelligence system with a continuous verified event stream
  • Source reliability scores learned from each outlet's accuracy and corrections record
  • Analysis of which news types came before the trader's best and worst trades; risk-regime detection
  • Hindi business press, voice interaction, fully auditable. A human stays in the loop; no auto-trading.
What it feels likeThe trader opens one app at 08:45 and trusts every line in it.

§11

Market-ready UX

UX rules drawn from ui-ux-pro-max (financial dashboard, data-dense, density 8/10, subtle motion) and adapted to a product whose main feature is trust.

Trust patterns

Proof on every surface

Citation superscripts on every claim. Three verification badges (Primary data · Verified · Attributed view). An as-of chip on every number. Source-tier chips. Visible withheld states. A changelog on corrected reports.

Freshness

Time is always visible

An IST clock and market-status pills in the top bar. As-of chips turn orange, with an icon and the word “stale”, once a figure passes its freshness window. Never colour alone.

States

Loading, empty, error, partial

Skeletons reserve space (CLS < 0.1). Empty states explain the rule, e.g. “No tech story met the two-source rule since 08:45.” Errors offer a way forward: “Feed B isn't responding. Showing NSE values only. Retry.”

Density

Dense, but scannable

12-column grid, max 1400 px, 13 px UI base, tabular numbers. Summary before detail: takeaways, then tiles, then tables. Indian digit grouping for ₹, standard grouping for index levels.

Colour & access

Up/down never colour-only

▲▼ glyphs and signs on every change; candles filled vs hollow; 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes; a table view for every chart; aria-live on breaking alerts.

Motion

Calm at 09:00

Transitions of 150–300 ms for state changes only. No scrolling tickers or flashing prices. Respects reduced-motion settings.

Speed

Keyboard-first

/ search · J/K next and previous story · C open citations · G then M go to Markets. Visible focus rings; 44 px touch targets on mobile.

Notifications

Few, verified, useful

Report-ready pushes plus verified breaking alerts, capped at 5 per day, with quiet hours and a digest option. Toasts auto-dismiss after 3–5 s; alerts stay until read.

Onboarding & pricing (SaaS)

Value before signup

A sample report without signup. Three skippable setup steps: instruments, report times and channels, watchlist. Three plans with the middle one highlighted and 20–30% off annual; the FAQ answers “Is this investment advice?” (No.)

Themes & screens

Dark-first, light supported

Dark by default for early-morning screens, with a full light theme. Breakpoints 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440; no horizontal scroll; bottom tab bar with 5 items on mobile.

Guardrail metrics

Guardrails protect trust while the success metrics push for speed and coverage. The first three are hard stops.

GuardrailThresholdIf breached
Unverified claims published0Sev-1 Pull the claim, publish a correction, freeze the rule or source, post-mortem within 24 h.
Fabricated or non-matching citations0Sev-1 Same as above, and add the case to the verifier's regression tests.
Numbers outside tolerance of primary data0Sev-1 Auto-withhold, alert, review the feed.
Stale numbers0 without a valid as-of timeBlock publishing that number.
Dead citation links0 at publish · < 2% after 7 daysServe the archived copy; replace the link.
Corrections≤ 1% of claims · fixed ≤ 30 min after a flagReview that source's tier.
Source concentrationNo outlet > 30% of a report's citationsRebalance collectors; syndicated copies count once.
Advice leakage0 buy/sell calls or targets in Citebell's voiceGate blocks those phrasings; review the template.
Late or missed reports> 15 min late ≤ 1 / month · missed = 0Incident review; add schedule buffer.
Alert fatigue≤ 5 pushes per trading dayFold extra alerts into a digest.

CostWithin the monthly LLM + data budgetUse cheaper models for non-critical steps; alert at 80%.

Appendix

Risks, assumptions, open questions, sources

Risks & mitigations

RiskWhy it mattersMitigation
Data licensingRedistributing exchange data, especially real-time, needs a licence; many news sites forbid scraping in their terms.Personal-use broker and licensed APIs in V0–V2; RSS and official feeds only; get an exchange or vendor licence before any SaaS launch.
RegulationCharging users for market analysis may bring in SEBI research-analyst or investment-adviser rules.Stay factual and attributed; no advice in Citebell's own voice; get legal advice before V3 billing.
LLM errorsA model can invent a number or a source.Numbers come from data, never generated text; independent verifier; fail-closed gate; regression set of known-tricky claims.
Source outages and delaysExchange files and feeds can arrive late.Second feed; withheld states with retry times; last verified values shown with their dates.
PaywallsSome claims can't be read in full.Cite only what's verifiable in the free portion, or use an alternative source.
Over-relianceA trusted tool can be followed blindly.Clear badges, attributed views, journaling prompts, “information, not advice” copy.
Name and trademarkA conflict discovered late forces a rebrand.Register domains now; formal trademark search before public launch.
Assumptions to validate
  • Current pre-market research takes 60–90 min (measure in V0).
  • The named outlets offer usable RSS or official feeds.
  • Provisional FII/DII data arrives in the evening; sample reports were timestamped between 18:59 and 19:40 IST.
  • A broker API gives index data for personal use at acceptable cost.
Open questions
  • Which broker API: Kite, Dhan or Upstox?
  • What monthly budget for data and LLM calls?
  • Delivery channel: Telegram, WhatsApp or email first?
  • Hindi business press in V1 or later?
  • The repo is public: should prompts and the source registry live in a private repo?
